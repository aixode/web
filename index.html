How it works

Simple to start.
Hard to put down.

Four steps to a healthier relationship with your phone.

1

Set your goals

Tell Aixode how much screen time you want each day. It adapts to you, not the other way around.

2

Aixode guides you

Aixode blocks your chosen apps when you hit your goal. No willpower required.

3

Set reminders for what matters

Create your own reminders for what matters to you, like family meals or quality time with the people you love.

4

Watch yourself grow

Track your streak, see weekly progress, and watch your screen-time habits improve over time.

Privacy & trust

Your data stays yours.

Aixode is built to help you, not to profit from your attention. We collect as little as possible and keep your screen-time data on your phone.

Stays on your device

Your screen-time and app usage data stays on your phone.

Only the basics

We only ask for your name, email and phone number. Nothing more.

Never sold

We never sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to anyone.

GDPR compliant
